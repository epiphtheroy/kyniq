**Subject 후보:**
- Five films worth your evening — judged, not averaged
- Tonight: an Ozu for free, a Dreyer in 82 minutes

---

Every Tuesday, five films — with the reason, the cost, and where they actually
stream. Scores here are Metatake's TakeScore: a 13-dimension critical estimate
of what a film gives a serious viewer, what it asks, and how it can fail — not
a popularity average. The judgments are mine.
