# The Cinephile's Master Taxonomy (source, user-provided)

> 보존용 원본. movement/style 어휘·멤버십·렉시콘의 정본. 통합 맵은 `../06_taxonomy_map.md`.

## I. Early Cinema & The Historical Avant-Garde
- **Cinema of Attractions** (pre-1907) — spectacle/novelty/direct address over narrative. Lumière Brothers, Méliès, Edwin S. Porter.
- **Brighton School** (UK, c.1900) — early close-up & continuity editing. G.A. Smith, James Williamson.
- **German Expressionism** (1920s) — chiaroscuro, distorted mise-en-scène. Wiene, Murnau, Lang (early).
- **Kammerspielfilm** (Germany, 1920s) — intimate psychological chamber-drama, anti-Expressionism. Murnau, Dreyer.
- **Soviet Montage** (1920s–30s) — dialectical editing. Eisenstein, Vertov, Pudovkin, Kuleshov.
- **French Impressionism / First Avant-Garde** (1920s) — subjective states, photogénie. Gance, Epstein, Dulac, Delluc.
- **Surrealist Cinema** (1920s–30s) — dream logic, shock. Buñuel, Dalí, Man Ray.
- **Cinéma Pur** (1920s) — abstract rhythm/form/motion. Clair, Léger, Chomette.

## II. Classical Era & Post-War Realisms
- **Poetic Realism** (France, 1930s) — lyrical fatalism. Renoir, Carné, Vigo, Duvivier.
- **Classical Hollywood / Studio System** (c.1920s–60s) — genre + invisible editing. Hawks, Ford, Welles, Hitchcock.
- **Tradition of Quality** (France) — polished literary studio films the New Wave attacked.
- **Italian Neorealism** (1940s–50s) — non-actors, location, working class. Rossellini, De Sica, Visconti.
- **Japanese Golden Age** (1950s) — Kurosawa, Ozu, Mizoguchi.

## III. Global New Waves & Post-War Modernism
- **French New Wave (Nouvelle Vague)** (late 1950s–60s) — Cahiers/Right Bank: Godard, Truffaut, Chabrol, Rohmer, Rivette.
- **Left Bank (Rive Gauche)** — Resnais, Varda, Marker, Demy.
- **New German Cinema (Neuer Deutscher Film)** (1960s–80s) — Fassbinder, Herzog, Wenders, Schlöndorff, Kluge.
- **Japanese New Wave (Nūberu Bāgu)** (late 1950s–70s) — Oshima, Imamura, Shinoda, Suzuki.
- **British New Wave / Kitchen Sink Realism** (late 1950s–60s) — Richardson, Reisz, Anderson.
- **New Hollywood (American New Wave)** (late 1960s–early 80s) — Penn, Scorsese, Coppola, Altman, Ashby, Malick.
- **Parallel Cinema** (India, 1950s+) — Ray, Mrinal Sen, Ghatak.

## IV. Eastern Bloc Cinema
- **Polish School** (1950s) — Wajda, Munk.
- **Czechoslovak New Wave** (1960s) — Forman, Chytilová, Menzel, Němec.
- **Yugoslav Black Wave (Crni Talas)** (1960s–early 70s) — Makavejev, Petrović, Žilnik.

## V. Political Radicalism & Decolonization
- **Cinema Novo** (Brazil, 1960s–70s) — "aesthetic of hunger." Glauber Rocha, Pereira dos Santos.
- **Third Cinema (Tercer Cine)** (Argentina/global, late 1960s+) — Solanas & Getino; Sembène.
- **Imperfect Cinema (Cine Imperfecto)** (Cuba) — García Espinosa, Gutiérrez Alea.
- **L.A. Rebellion** (USA, 1970s–80s) — Burnett, Dash, Gerima, Woodberry.

## VI. East Asian Cinematic Renaissances
- **Taiwanese New Cinema** (1980s+) — Hou Hsiao-hsien, Edward Yang (Tsai Ming-liang = Second Wave).
- **Chinese Fifth Generation** (1980s) — Zhang Yimou, Chen Kaige, Tian Zhuangzhuang.
- **Chinese Sixth Generation (Urban Generation)** (1990s+) — Jia Zhangke, Wang Xiaoshuai, Lou Ye.
- **Hong Kong New Wave** (late 1970s–90s) — 1st: Tsui Hark, Ann Hui; 2nd: Wong Kar-wai, Stanley Kwan.
- **Korean New Wave** (late 1990s+) — Lee Chang-dong, Hong Sang-soo, Bong Joon-ho, Park Chan-wook.

## VII. Late 20th Century, Postmodernism, New Voices
- **Cinéma du Look** (France, 1980s) — Besson, Beineix, Carax.
- **Iranian New Wave** (1980s+) — Kiarostami, Makhmalbaf, Panahi.
- **New Queer Cinema** (early 1990s) — Haynes, Van Sant, Jarman, Araki.
- **Dogme 95** (Denmark, 1995) — Vow of Chastity. von Trier, Vinterberg.

## VIII. Contemporary Global Cinema (Cutting Edge)
- **Slow Cinema / Contemplative Cinema** — long takes, duration. Tarr, Lav Diaz, Apichatpong, Pedro Costa, Lisandro Alonso, Akerman. [→ also `style`]
- **Romanian New Wave** (mid-2000s+) — Puiu, Mungiu, Porumboiu, Jude.
- **Greek Weird Wave** (late 2000s+) — Lanthimos, Tsangari.
- **Berlin School (Berliner Schule)** (2000s+) — Petzold, Schanelec, Ade.
- **New French Extremity** (late 1990s–2000s) — Noé, Denis, Breillat, Dumont.
- **Mumblecore** (USA, 2000s) — Bujalski, Swanberg, Duplass Brothers.
- **New Argentine Cinema (Nuevo Cine Argentino)** (late 1990s+) — Martel, Alonso, Rejtman.

## IX. Experimental, Avant-Garde, Underground
- **New American Cinema / Underground** (1950s–60s) — Deren, Brakhage, Mekas, Warhol, Anger, Jack Smith.
- **Structural/Materialist Film** (1960s–70s) — Snow, Frampton, Sharits, Conrad.
- **No Wave Cinema** (NYC, late 1970s–early 80s) — Jarmusch (early), Amos Poe, Vivienne Dick.
- **Expanded Cinema** — multi-screen/installation/performance.
- **Zanzibar Group** (late 1960s, obscure) — Garrel (early).

## X. Documentary Modes & Movements
- **Direct Cinema** (USA/Canada) — fly-on-the-wall. Wiseman, Maysles, Pennebaker.
- **Cinéma Vérité** (France) — participatory. Rouch, Marker.
- **The Essay Film** — subjective/reflexive. Marker, Farocki, Varda, Steyerl.
- **Harvard Sensory Ethnography Lab (SEL)** — Castaing-Taylor, Paravel.
- **Desktop Documentary** — narrative on a computer screen.

## XI. Genre Dynamics & Revisionism
- **Film Noir** (classic 1940s–50s; Neo-Noir later) — fatalism, chiaroscuro, femme fatale.
- **Spaghetti Western (Western all'italiana)** (1960s) — Leone, Corbucci.
- **Giallo** (Italy, 1960s–70s) — Bava, Argento.
- **Pinku Eiga (Pink Film)** (Japan) — Wakamatsu.
- **Elevated Horror** (contemporary, debated) — Aster, Eggers, Peele (A24).

## XII. Critical Concepts & Stylistic Descriptors (Lexicon)
- **Auteur Theory** — director as primary author. [노이즈성, 드롭 권장]
- **Bressonian** — austere minimalism, non-actors "models" (Bresson).
- **Hyperlink Cinema / Network Narrative** — intersecting storylines. Altman, Iñárritu.
- **Lynchian** — surreal uncanny, mundane+grotesque (Lynch).
- **Metamodernism** — oscillation of earnestness/irony.
- **Ozu-esque** — static low tatami-shot contemplation (Ozu).
- **Post-Cinema** — digital/surveillance/algorithmic moving images supersede cinema.
- **Tableau Cinema** — static painterly compositions. Greenaway, Roy Andersson.
- **Tarkovskian** — long takes, metaphysical, elemental (Tarkovsky).
- **Vulgar Auteurism** — auteurist analysis of "low-brow"/genre directors. Bay, Tony Scott, P.W.S. Anderson.
