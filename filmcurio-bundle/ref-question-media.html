What does the blue box actually mean?

asked by cinephile_92 · 3 days ago
The reading

The box is the seam between Diane's fantasy and the unbearable real — to open it is to end the dream and fall back into the life she could not live.

Honestly, Lynch never shows you an inside — open the box and it's just dark, not a place. That's the whole trick. The box is the edge of the wish, the line past which the dream can't keep protecting her. The second it opens, the movie's grammar snaps and the crueler film begins.
