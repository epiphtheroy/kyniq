Is the Zone a real place with strange powers, an alien landscape, or a metaphor for the characters' inner lives?

asked by FilmCurio Editorial · today

The film keeps the Zone's nature suspended on purpose. How much is external reality, and how much is the men's own hope and disillusion looking back at them?

The reading

The Zone is real enough to kill you and unreal enough to read your mind — and Tarkovsky refuses to resolve which. The long, unbroken takes make you do the walking the characters do, so the landscape's "powers" arrive as attention, not effects. By the time the Room is in reach, the question has quietly migrated from what the Zone is to what the men are afraid it will show them.
