What does the blue box actually mean?

asked by cinephile_92 · 3 days ago
The reading

The box is the seam between Diane's fantasy and the unbearable real — to open it is to end the dream and fall back into the life she could not live.

Lynch withholds an interior: the blue inside is darkness, not a place. That refusal is the point — the box marks the limit of the wish, the threshold past which the dream can no longer protect her. When it opens, the film's grammar breaks and the second, crueler movie begins.
