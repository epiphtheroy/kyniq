the-power-of-the-dog-2021 · 10 figures · 30 takes

Jane Campion's *The Power of the Dog* (2021) is a slow-burn psychological thriller disguised as a Western. It continues to spark debate for its surgical dismantling of frontier mythology and its chilling exploration of repressed desire, performative masculinity, and the lethal lengths of queer survival.
