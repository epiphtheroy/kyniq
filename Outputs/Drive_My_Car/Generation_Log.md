## PHASE 1 — 8-PARAGRAPH BLUEPRINT

- **P1 — Opening Hook.** The red Saab, the three hours, a man who would rather be driven than arrive. *Core:* introduce the premise through its strangest fact — a car as the film's true theatre. *Bait (Chekhov):* a recorded voice that is more alive in the room than anyone breathing. *Transition:* who put that voice on tape, and why he can't switch it off.
- **P2 — The unmourned wife.** Oto, the affair, her sudden death, the conversation Kafuku ducked. *Core:* his grief is not sorrow but stoppage — the lost thing swallowed whole, kept running. *Source:* readings 5, 7. *Transition:* the form that stoppage takes — a tape.
- **P3 — The acousmatic voice.** Oto reading Vanya on cassette; the soul kept while the letter rots. *Core:* the truest presence in the film has no body. *Source:* reading 1; neighbor Aftersun. *Transition:* the other text she leaves him — the play itself.
- **P4 — The play that invades.** Uncle Vanya as a slow machine that says what the man can't; emotionless read-throughs; the text leaking into life. *Core:* theatre as delayed confession. *Source:* readings 3, 6; Renoir/Husserl. *Transition:* where the rehearsal's truth finally gets spoken — the car.
- **P5 — The mobile confessional.** Why truth needs motion and no eye contact; Misaki at the wheel; the threshold space. *Core:* intimacy at an angle, never face to face. *Source:* reading 2; Kiarostami lineage. *Transition:* except for one face that needs no words.
- **P6 — Language and the body.** Multilingual Vanya; Korean Sign Language; meaning beneath words; Hamaguchi's faith in pre-verbal communion. *Core:* the film trusts hands and silence over speech. *Source:* reading 4; Levinas. *Transition:* and if you ask what this resembles, the answer surprises.
- **P7 — Family resemblances.** Adaptation that betrays the plot to keep the soul; the unlikely cousins (Burning, even the brash ones); duration as the real subject. *Core:* "spirit over letter," and a cinema of time. *Source:* neighbors; Bazin dead time; Deleuze. *Transition:* all roads lead north, to snow.
- **P8 — Arrival (Hokkaido).** The ruined house, the confession to Misaki, the tape laid to rest; the journey that was the point. *Core:* mourning completed only by being driven through it. *Hook:* pay off the bait — the voice finally given a body to rest in, the canonized film that has fully arrived. *Sew to P1.*
