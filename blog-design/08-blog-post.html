Between Film and the World

Five things that happened, and the films that already knew.

Thursday, June 18, 20266 min readThe Metatake desk

Every day we read the wire the way we read a film: looking for the figure underneath. These are the day's events that rhymed hardest with something in the Metatake corpus — every film and reading below was confirmed in the live database before we linked it. Ranked by the strength of the rhyme. What didn't earn it is at the bottom.

1

A former military island is handed to a private resort.

Sazan Island, Albania→Bacurau★★★★★rhyme

For a 12th straight day, tens of thousands of Albanians marched against the Kushner-Trump luxury resort planned for the protected Vjosa-Narta lagoon and Sazan Island — a former communist-era military base — as the anticorruption prosecutor opened an inquiry into how the land's protected status was quietly changed.

The figure isn't "a hotel." It's enclosure — common ground (and a flamingo lagoon) reclassified as someone's private island. Cinema's fiercest image of it is Bacurau, whose village is scrubbed from the map so foreign visitors can treat it as a playground; Metatake reads it through The State of Exception — the move where the usual rules are suspended for the powerful. The film's reply isn't a petition. It's the village remembering it outnumbers its guests.

→In Metatake: deposits the enclosed commons, joining today's island to The State of Exception and to the land-buyer of There Will Be Blood.

Bacurau · 2019
2

An emperor performs a sovereignty he does not hold.

Naruhito's state visit→The Last Emperor★★★★★rhyme

The Netherlands gave Emperor Naruhito and Empress Masako a full state welcome at Dam Square — wreath, anthems, gold braid — for a reign that is, by Japan's own constitution, entirely ceremonial.

The figure is power drained into pure role. Bertolucci's Puyi is crowned as a child over a country he will never govern, and Metatake gathers that into the reading Selfhood Dissolved Into a Role — the person vanishing into the office. Naruhito's wave from the square is that story's gentle, democratic ending: the crown survives precisely by becoming, at last, only a performance.

→In Metatake: deposits sovereignty-as-ritual, joining the state visit and Puyi's hollow throne to Selfhood Dissolved Into a Role.

The Last Emperor · 1987

3

At thirty-eight, he ties the all-time record.

Messi's World Cup hat-trick→The Wrestler★★★★★rhyme

Lionel Messi, 38, scored a hat-trick in Argentina's 3-0 win over Algeria — 20 years to the day after his World Cup debut — drawing level with Miroslav Klose's all-time record of 16 World Cup goals.

The rhyme for the maestro at the far end isn't a sports movie. Metatake's reading of Randy "The Ram," Flesh Reduced to Persistence, rests on a body kept going past sense, wholly itself only in the ring. Messi is the luminous inverse — the body still obeys, the genius still arrives on time — but the figure underneath is shared: the performer for whom the arena is the one room where the clock agrees to wait.

→In Metatake: deposits the body that performs past its hour, joining Messi's day to Flesh Reduced to Persistence, across to Raging Bull.

The Wrestler · 2008
4

A seventy-year-old bomber falls out of the sky.

The Edwards B-52 crash→Top Gun: Maverick★★★★★rhyme

A B-52 went down after takeoff from Edwards Air Force Base on a radar-modernization test flight; all eight aboard were killed — a real loss, named first.

The figure is the machine we will not retire. The B-52 first flew in 1952 and is being upgraded to serve into the 2050s. Top Gun: Maverick's entire plot is an institution trying to ground a pilot and a way of flying the drone age has made obsolete, and Metatake files it under Preemptive Nostalgia — mourning a thing even as we cling to it. The movie lets the old machine win one more time. Thursday was the other ending.

→In Metatake: deposits the machine kept past its era, joining the airframe to Preemptive Nostalgia, near the un-buildable deterrent of Oppenheimer.

Top Gun: Maverick · 2022
5

The season's first storm gets a name, then fades.

Tropical Storm Arthur→Melancholia★★★★★rhyme

Arthur, the first named storm of the 2026 Atlantic season, formed off the Texas Gulf Coast and then weakened to a post-tropical cyclone — the year's opening notice that more are coming.

The figure is the named catastrophe on the horizon. We give storms friendly names to make the impersonal feel negotiable; von Trier names the approaching planet too, and Metatake reads Melancholia through The Indifferent Landscape — a world serenely uninterested in our dread. The fear was never the impact. It's the blue-sky interval before it, when the end is still just a small swirl with a friendly name.

→In Metatake: deposits the named catastrophe, joining storm-naming to The Indifferent Landscape, beside The Tree of Life.

Melancholia · 2011
