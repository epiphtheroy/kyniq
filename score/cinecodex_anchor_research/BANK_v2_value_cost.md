# Cinecodex Anchor Bank v2 — VALUE & COST

**Status:** Consolidated v2 — reconciled against VB1 adversarial review
**Reconciliation editor:** Layer C
**Date:** 2026-06-29
**Scope:** VALUE (COG · AFF · FORM · MORAL · DUR) + COST (ITX · FR · ETX · CTX)

> **Two reading rules carried over from v1, reaffirmed here:**
> 1. **VALUE axes are independent.** A high score on one does not imply a high score on another. (Shoah anchors both COG 100 and AFF 100 by independent evidence.)
> 2. **COST ≠ VALUE.** The cost axis measures the *entry toll*, never the reward. A hard film can be empty; an easy film can be transcendent.
>
> **Anchor-safety rule (new in v2):** anchors must be CONSENSUAL. Divisive / love-it-or-hate-it films (Tree of Life, Dancer in the Dark, Funny Games, Son of Saul, Joker) have been removed from anchor slots and belong on the POLAR axis, handled elsewhere.

# VALUE AXIS

## COG — Cognitive Yield
*New ways of thinking or perceiving that persist after viewing. 0 = nothing new → 100 = lasting conceptual/perceptual shift.*
*Calibration note: legibility is NOT penalized. Clear ideas delivered conventionally = COG 50. Difficulty is an access-cost matter (A3), not a cognitive-value matter.*

---

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
