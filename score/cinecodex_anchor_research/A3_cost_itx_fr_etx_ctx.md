# A3: ACCESS COST AXIS — Anchor Film Research
## Cinecodex Sub-dimensions: ITX · FR · ETX · CTX

> **CRITICAL FRAMING NOTE**: Access cost is a PREREQUISITE burden placed on the viewer — not a measure of quality, depth, or merit. A difficult, empty film may score high on all four dimensions while being low-value. A universally accessible film may be a masterpiece. This axis measures the *entry toll*, never the *reward inside*. High cost ≠ high value. A film can be hard and worthless, or easy and transcendent.

---

## ITX — Intertextuality
**Definition:** Required knowledge of film history, genre conventions, and film theory to unlock the film's intended meaning. 0 = no cinematic prior knowledge needed; 100 = encyclopedic mastery of film history mandatory.

---

### ITX = 0 — No film knowledge needed; universally accessible

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Parasite* (2019, Bong Joon-ho) | Pure class-war thriller; zero genre prerequisites, universally legible |
| *Bicycle Thieves* (1948, De Sica) | Working-class human drama; no cinematic frame required to feel it |
| *Pather Panchali* (1955, Ray) | Family poverty story; visceral emotion needs no film-historical scaffolding |
| *Pan's Labyrinth* (2006, del Toro) | Fantasy/war; fairy-tale grammar every viewer already carries |
| *A Separation* (2011, Farhadi) | Legal/domestic drama; accessible moral stakes, no genre codes required |
| *Spirited Away* (2001, Miyazaki) | Children's quest; universal mythic structure, no cinephile entry fee |

---

### ITX = 25 — Mild genre awareness helps, but not required

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *The Good, the Bad and the Ugly* (1966, Leone) | Richer with Western genre awareness; still viscerally enjoyable without it |
| *Get Out* (2017, Peele) | Horror conventions amplify dread; works as thriller for genre-naive viewers |
| *Chinatown* (1974, Polanski) | Noir callbacks reward genre knowledge; plot is self-sufficient without them |
| *Wild Strawberries* (1957, Bergman) | Art-house dream logic mild; emotional arc legible without theory background |
| *Yi Yi* (2000, Yang) | Long-take Taiwanese drama; slight patience required, no intertextual debt |
| *Chungking Express* (1994, Wong Kar-wai) | Pop culture references surface-level; mood carries first-time viewers fully |

---

### ITX = 50 — Knowledge of specific genre traditions enhances significantly

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Breathless* (1960, Godard) | Rewrites American noir codes; knowing Bogart makes the theft audible |
| *Once Upon a Time in the West* (1968, Leone) | Eulogizes the Western genre; dense with classical-Western iconographic quotation |
| *Mulholland Drive* (2001, Lynch) | Hollywood dream-machine critique; knowing noir and musical tropes matters greatly |
| *Talk to Her* (2002, Almodóvar) | Melodrama tradition required to read its emotional registers accurately |
| *The Long Goodbye* (1973, Altman) | Systematic deconstruction of Chandler/Marlowe; rewards genre familiarity hugely |
| *Certified Copy* (2010, Kiarostami) | Rossellini's *Journey to Italy* is the silent intertext; awareness doubles meaning |

---

### ITX = 75 — Substantial film history/theory knowledge required to unlock meaning

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *8½* (1963, Fellini) | Self-portrait of a director; auteur theory and neorealism context required |
| *Persona* (1966, Bergman) | Identity/medium essay; film-theory literacy is the admission ticket |
| *The Passenger* (1975, Antonioni) | Modernist identity crisis; Antonioni's trilogy and existentialist cinema are the key |
| *Contempt / Le Mépris* (1963, Godard) | Three-layer citation: Homer, Fritz Lang, Hollywood; film history debt is large |
| *Sans Soleil* (1983, Marker) | Essay film built from film history citations; *Vertigo* is structural bedrock |
| *Nouvelle Vague* (2025, Linklater) | Depicts making of *Breathless*; French New Wave mastery required throughout |

Sources: [Criterion — Nouvelle Vague](https://www.criterion.com/films/35769-nouvelle-vague) · [Screening the Past — Histoire(s)](https://www.screeningthepast.com/issue-34-untimely-cinema/a-skeleton-key-to-histoires-du-cinema/)

---

### ITX = 100 — Encyclopedic/meta-cinematic; the film IS film history; mastery required

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Histoire(s) du cinéma* (1988–98, Godard) | The Finnegans Wake of cinema; 266-min dense citation of the entire film century |
| *The Image Book / Le Livre d'Image* (2018, Godard) | Requires mastery of Godard's 60-year oeuvre plus all of world cinema |
| *Céline and Julie Go Boating* (1974, Rivette) | Structurally dismantles narrative film; rewards encyclopedic genre and theory mastery |
| *Film Socialisme* (2010, Godard) | Assumes lifelong cinema engagement as precondition; otherwise fully opaque |
| *Allemagne 90 neuf zéro* (1991, Godard) | Political-cinematic essay; requires fluency in both European film history and politics |
| *Out 1: Noli Me Tangere* (1971, Rivette) | 13-hour improvised Balzac/cinema essay; demands full French New Wave immersion |

Sources: [Histoire(s) — MoMA](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/130055) · [Senses of Cinema — Godard](https://www.sensesofcinema.com/2001/garrel-godard-pt-2/godard_horwath/) · [Radiance Films](https://www.radiancefilms.co.uk/products/histoires-du-cinema-and-other-works-le)

## FR — Formal Radicalism
**Definition:** Distance from mainstream/Hollywood narrative grammar (nonlinearity, duration, anti-narrative, structural negation). 0 = classical/accessible grammar; 100 = avant-garde structural negation, pure-form, endurance-testing.

---

### FR = 0 — Classical/accessible narrative grammar

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Parasite* (2019, Bong Joon-ho) | Genre-clean three-act structure; classical causality, zero formal departure |
| *Bicycle Thieves* (1948, De Sica) | Linear single-day quest; Hollywood grammar in neorealist clothes |
| *Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon* (2000, Lee) | Genre spectacle with conventional editing rhythm and linear causality |
| *Roma* (2018, Cuarón) | Long takes but classical chronological narrative; patience, not formal radicalism |
| *City of God* (2002, Meirelles) | Non-linear but TV-thriller grammar; MTV editing, zero formal alienation |
| *Grave of the Fireflies* (1988, Takahata) | Linear chronological drama; emotionally devastating, formally conventional |

---

### FR = 25 — Mild departures; art-house accessible

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Wild Strawberries* (1957, Bergman) | Dream sequences break linearity gently; accessible with minimal patience adjustment |
| *Y Tu Mamá También* (2001, Cuarón) | Intrusive narrator, loose episodic structure; mild formal departure from norm |
| *Yi Yi* (2000, Yang) | Long takes and quiet tempo; art-house pacing, not alienating grammar |
| *Portrait of a Lady on Fire* (2019, Sciamma) | Deliberate pacing, mild patience; classical scene construction retained throughout |
| *Three Colors: Blue* (1993, Kieślowski) | Fragmented grief; emotionally elliptical but formal grammar stays accessible |
| *The Taste of Cherry* (1997, Kiarostami) | Repetitive structure signals art-house; still causally grounded, mildly demanding |

---

### FR = 50 — Noticeable departures from Hollywood grammar; demands active patience

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Jeanne Dielman, 23 quai du Commerce* (1975, Akerman) | Domestic repetition as formal act; duration and silence are the argument |
| *Last Year at Marienbad* (1961, Resnais) | Irresolvable time-space; Hollywood causality fully suspended, requires surrender |
| *Stalker* (1979, Tarkovsky) | Extremely long takes, allegory over plot; patience is a structural requirement |
| *In the Mood for Love* (2000, Wong Kar-wai) | Elliptical, suppressed causality; repetition and withholding replace conventional exposition |
| *Uncle Boonmee Who Can Recall His Past Lives* (2010, Weerasethakul) | Thai ghost-visitation structure; dream/waking blended without Western causal logic |
| *Caché / Hidden* (2005, Haneke) | Long static takes; refused resolution deliberately alienates narrative expectation |

Sources: [BFI — Jeanne Dielman](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/features/greatest-film-all-time-jeanne-dielman-23-quai-du-commerce-1080-bruxelles) · [Criterion — Last Year at Marienbad](https://www.criterion.com/films/1517-last-year-at-marienbad)

---

### FR = 75 — Strongly anti-narrative, very slow; endurance-testing

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Sátántangó* (1994, Béla Tarr) | 7.5-hour palindromic structure; endurance of mud, time, and political hopelessness |
| *The Turin Horse* (2011, Béla Tarr) | 6 days repeating in 30-minute long takes; duration IS the content |
| *Tropical Malady* (2004, Weerasethakul) | Bifurcates mid-film; second half abandons narrative grammar entirely |
| *Horse Money* (2014, Pedro Costa) | Hallucinatory duration; stationary figures in darkness — narrative nearly absent |
| *Norte, the End of History* (2013, Lav Diaz) | 4-hour Filipino epic; extreme long-take, very slow anti-narrative rhythm |
| *Slow Action* (2011, Ben Rivers) | Four-island ethnographic fictions; minimal causality, duration as primary language |

Sources: [Slant — Sátántangó](https://www.slantmagazine.com/film/satantango-hungary-1994-bela-tarr/) · [Offscreen — Lav Diaz](https://offscreen.com/view/stories-that-remain-an-interview-with-lav-diaz-by-paul-douglas-grant)

---

### FR = 100 — Avant-garde/structural negation; pure form; total reorientation required

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Wavelength* (1967, Michael Snow) | 45-min single zoom; structurally negates all narrative in favor of pure perception |
| *Zorns Lemma* (1970, Hollis Frampton) | Alphabetical image-structure replaces story; the system itself is the sole content |
| *La Région Centrale* (1971, Michael Snow) | 3-hour remote-controlled camera sweep of empty terrain; anti-human structural cinema |
| *Out 1: Noli Me Tangere* (1971, Jacques Rivette) | 13-hour improvisation; duration as structural negation of cinematic convention |
| *Empire* (1964, Andy Warhol) | 8-hour fixed shot of the Empire State Building; pure duration, zero narrative |
| *The Clock* (2010, Christian Marclay) | 24-hour found-footage: one real-time minute = one clip; the rule is the film |

Sources: [Wikipedia — Wavelength](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wavelength_(1967_film)) · [Wikipedia — Experimental Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_film)

## ETX — Extratextuality
**Definition:** Required external knowledge (history, politics, culture, philosophy) to unlock meaning. 0 = universal themes; 100 = specialized field knowledge mandatory — opaque without it.

---

### ETX = 0 — Universal themes; no external knowledge required

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Bicycle Thieves* (1948, De Sica) | Father-son poverty and loss; need requires no cultural glossary |
| *My Neighbor Totoro* (1988, Miyazaki) | Childhood wonder and grief; emotionally transparent across all cultures |
| *The Kid* (1921, Chaplin) | Orphan, poverty, love; silent visual comedy fully self-explanatory |
| *Spirited Away* (2001, Miyazaki) | Child-in-underworld archetypes carry fully without Shinto knowledge |
| *Ikiru* (1952, Kurosawa) | Bureaucrat facing death; meaning-of-life theme entirely self-contained |
| *Supa Modo* (2018, Likarion Wainaina) | Rural Kenyan childhood, terminal illness; grief and community need no decoder |

---

### ETX = 25 — Light cultural context enriches but is not mandatory

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Parasite* (2019, Bong Joon-ho) | Korean "semi-basement" poverty specifics enrich; class satire works without it |
| *Chungking Express* (1994, Wong Kar-wai) | 1997 Hong Kong handover deepens reading; film functions fully without it |
| *Wild Strawberries* (1957, Bergman) | Swedish Lutheran guilt adds resonance; narrative comprehension is complete without it |
| *Y Tu Mamá También* (2001, Cuarón) | Mexican class geography enriches subtext; road-trip story fully legible alone |
| *Capernaum* (2018, Labaki) | Lebanese statelessness gives context; child suffering story universally decodable |
| *Grave of the Fireflies* (1988, Takahata) | WWII Japan setting helps; sibling survival tragedy needs no prior history |

---

### ETX = 50 — Historical/cultural knowledge significantly aids comprehension

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Sátántangó* (1994, Béla Tarr) | Post-communist Hungarian village collapse; 1980s Soviet bloc decay is near-required context |
| *The Battle of Chile* (1975–79, Guzmán) | Allende coup and aftermath; 1973 coup knowledge needed to parse political stakes |
| *Once Upon a Time in Anatolia* (2011, Ceylan) | Turkey's secular/Islamic tension and rural bureaucracy materially deepen reading |
| *Caché / Hidden* (2005, Haneke) | Algeria's 1961 Paris massacre underpins the guilt structure; without it the film is merely mysterious |
| *Yol* (1982, Güney) | Kurdish oppression, 1980 military coup; the "road" metaphor collapses without context |
| *The White Ribbon* (2009, Haneke) | Pre-WWI German Protestant rural community; fascism-origin allegory requires knowing what follows |

Sources: [Béla Tarr Guide](https://cinemawavesblog.com/film-blog/where-begin-with-bela-tarr-guide/) · [Senses of Cinema — Güney](https://www.sensesofcinema.com/2005/great-directors/guney/) · [The Culturium — Ceylan](https://www.theculturium.com/nuri-bilge-ceylan-once-upon-a-time-in-anatolia/)

---

### ETX = 75 — Specialized political history, philosophy, or ethnography nearly mandatory

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Evolution of a Filipino Family* (2004, Lav Diaz) | Marcos martial law, U.S.-backed counterinsurgency: without this history, film is opaque |
| *Shoah* (1985, Lanzmann) | Holocaust historiography required to parse testimonial structure and absence of archival footage |
| *Jeanne Dielman* (1975, Akerman) | Without second-wave feminist materialist framework, 200-min routine is opaque not political |
| *Platform* (2000, Jia Zhangke) | 1980s Chinese socialism-to-capitalism shift; without it the temporal structure is a non-event |
| *La Commune (Paris, 1871)* (2000, Peter Watkins) | 1871 Paris Commune required; anachronistic TV-cameras conceit fails without knowing the event |
| *The Gospel According to Matthew* (1964, Pasolini) | Pasolini's Marxist-atheist contradictions are load-bearing; film opaque without his ideological position |

Sources: [Boston Review — Lav Diaz](https://www.bostonreview.net/articles/lav-diazs-quiet-storm/) · [Criterion — Shoah](https://www.criterion.com/films/27968-shoah) · [BFI — Jeanne Dielman](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/features/keeping-distance-chantal-akerman-jeanne-dielman) · [Offscreen — Jia Zhangke](https://offscreen.com/view/postsocialist_grit) · [Word on Fire — Pasolini](https://www.wordonfire.org/articles/pasolini-and-the-gospel-according-to-matthew/)

### ETX = 100 — Specialized field knowledge mandatory; film is categorically opaque without it

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Salò, or the 120 Days of Sodom* (1975, Pasolini) | Requires: de Sade's 120 Days, Salò Republic 1943–45, Pasolini's Marxist capitalism-as-fascism thesis, Bataille's eroticism-death nexus — all four simultaneously load-bearing |
| *Moses und Aron* (1975, Straub-Huillet) | Schoenberg's twelve-tone system + Old Testament unrepresentability theology + Brechtian anti-illusionism — all three required |
| *Introduction to Arnold Schoenberg's Accompaniment* (1973, Straub-Huillet) | Schoenberg's letters to Kandinsky, Brecht's capitalism-fascism thesis, atrocity footage: three specialist discourses simultaneously mandatory |
| *Histoire(s) du cinéma* (1988–98, Godard) | Encyclopedic cinema history + European thought (Benjamin, Malraux) + Godard's complete filmography: all load-bearing |
| *The Mirror* (1975, Tarkovsky) | Russian Orthodox spirituality, Soviet poetry (Arseny Tarkovsky), Soviet wartime memory: all structurally load-bearing for autobiographical montage |

Sources: [Artforum — Moses und Aron](https://www.artforum.com/features/seeing-ideas-straub-and-huillets-moses-und-aron-209354/) · [Senses of Cinema — Straub-Huillet](https://www.sensesofcinema.com/2019/jean-marie-straub-daniele-huillet/straub-huillet-brecht-and-the-two-avant-gardes/) · [White Fox Cinema — Tarkovsky](https://whitefoxcinema.com/news/A_Look_at_Andrei_Tarkovsky_cinema_A_Poetic_Narrative_of_Experience_Time_and_Ethics)

## CTX — Intratextuality
**Definition:** Reliance on the director's oeuvre and recurring motifs to unlock meaning. 0 = fully standalone; 100 = sequential mastery of complete filmography required — comprehension is nullified, not merely diminished, without it.

---

### CTX = 0 — Standalone; no prior oeuvre knowledge needed

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Parasite* (2019, Bong Joon-ho) | Genre-legible thriller; zero knowledge of Bong's prior films required |
| *The Battle of Algiers* (1966, Pontecorvo) | One-off; no director oeuvre context applies whatsoever |
| *Bicycle Thieves* (1948, De Sica) | Neorealism context enriches; narrative is absolutely self-contained |
| *Shoah* (1985, Lanzmann) | First and only film of its kind; no prior Lanzmann films exist |
| *Amour* (2012, Haneke) | Widely received as fully accessible without knowing Haneke's confrontational prior work |
| *Capernaum* (2018, Labaki) | Debut-scale public reception; no oeuvre dependency exists |

---

### CTX = 25 — Benefits from knowing director's style; self-contained

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *In the Mood for Love* (2000, Wong Kar-wai) | Knowing WKW's recurring emotional restraint enriches; film fully self-contained |
| *The White Ribbon* (2009, Haneke) | Knowing Haneke's Glaciation Trilogy sharpens reading; not a prerequisite |
| *Once Upon a Time in Anatolia* (2011, Ceylan) | Prior Ceylan small-town films (Distant, Climates) enrich; not prerequisite |
| *Songs from the Second Floor* (2000, Andersson) | First Living Trilogy installment; inaugurates the style, fully self-standing |
| *Uncle Boonmee* (2010, Weerasethakul) | Ghost/forest motifs reward oeuvre knowledge; confirmed accessible without prior films |
| *Yol* (1982, Güney) | Prior prison films form context; Yol stands as a complete statement |

Sources: [Britannica — Apichatpong](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Apichatpong_Weerasethakul)

---

### CTX = 50 — Prior films in series/oeuvre significantly aid comprehension

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *2046* (2004, Wong Kar-wai) | Direct sequel to *In the Mood for Love*; Chow Mo-wan's state is a continuation |
| *Saraband* (2003, Bergman) | Quasi-sequel to *Scenes from a Marriage* (1974); 30-year prior deepens Johan/Marianne |
| *You, the Living* (2007, Andersson) | Second Living Trilogy film; tableau grammar established in Film 1 is assumed |
| *Syndromes and a Century* (2006, Weerasethakul) | Bipartite structure and recurring motifs connect across oeuvre; benefits from *Tropical Malady* |
| *BRD Trilogy: Veronika Voss* (1982, Fassbinder) | Third BRD film; Maria Braun / Lola comparative ironies lost without sequence |
| *Through the Olive Trees* (1994, Kiarostami) | Meta-film set during making of film #2; without prior films the meta-premise collapses |

Sources: [Collider — 2046](https://collider.com/2046-wong-kar-wai/) · [Bergman official — Saraband](https://www.ingmarbergman.se/en/production/saraband) · [Criterion Channel — Koker Trilogy](https://www.criterionchannel.com/the-koker-trilogy) · [Wikipedia — BRD Trilogy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BRD_Trilogy) · [BFI — Apichatpong](https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/where-begin-with-apichatpong-weerasethakul)

### CTX = 75 — Strong sequential dependence; watching order is structurally meaningful

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *A Pigeon Sat on a Branch Reflecting on Existence* (2014, Andersson) | Third Living Trilogy film; philosophical payload requires full trilogy accumulation |
| *Histoire(s) du cinéma* (1988–98, Godard) | Constantly quotes Godard's prior work; self-citations are noise without filmography knowledge |
| *Film Socialisme* (2010, Godard) | Late-Godard essay mode assumes total command of post-1968 production; disorienting by design |
| *Right Now, Wrong Then* (2015, Hong Sang-soo) | Parallelism/variation pleasure requires recognizing repeated situation-types across Hong's oeuvre |
| *To the Wonder* (2012, Malick) | Critics consistently: appears incomplete without *The Tree of Life* scaffolding |
| *Passion* (1982, Godard) | Directly continues and cross-references *Every Man for Himself* (1980); meta-commentary on reinvention period |

### CTX = 100 — Sequential mastery of complete filmography required; comprehension is nullified without it

| Film | Reason (≤12 words) |
|---|---|
| *Histoire(s) du cinéma* (1988–98, Godard) | Treats Godard's entire filmography as raw material; without it, the film is incomprehensible |
| *Out 1: Noli Me Tangere* (1971, Rivette) | 13-hour film decoding requires full command of Rivette's theatrical and cinematic apparatus |
| *Oki's Movie* (2010, Hong Sang-soo) | Four chapters explicitly recycling archetypes and locations from eight prior films |
| *The Mirror* (1975, Tarkovsky) | Auto-biographical non-linear structure cites motifs from full Tarkovsky universe; partial knowledge fails |
| *The Image Book* (2018, Godard) | Final Godard; decoding requires his 60-year oeuvre plus world cinema history mastery |

## 고민/결정 노트

### 핵심 원칙 재확인
**비용 ≠ 가치.** 이 축의 가장 큰 함정은 높은 점수를 '훌륭함'의 표지로 읽는 것이다. *Histoire(s) du cinéma*는 ITX·ETX·CTX 전 영역에서 100점에 가깝지만, 그것이 *Bicycle Thieves*보다 위대하다는 의미가 아니다. 어렵고 공허한 영화도 100점을 받을 수 있다. 어렵고 충만한 영화도 마찬가지다. 이 점수는 입장료(entry toll)를 측정하는 것이지 내부의 보상을 측정하는 것이 아니다.

### 배치 결정 노트

**ITX 75 vs. 100 경계선**: *8½*와 *Persona*는 75에 배치했다. 이 두 편은 영화 이론적 리터러시를 '요구'하지만, 오토 생활의 서사적 코어가 이론 없이도 감정적으로 작동한다. 반면 *Histoire(s) du cinéma*는 영화사 지식 없이는 감정적으로도 서사적으로도 작동하지 않는다 — 그것이 100의 기준이다.

**FR에서 *Jeanne Dielman* 50 배치**: 비공식적 합의는 이 영화를 FR=100에 놓는 경향이 있다. 그러나 Akerman의 영화는 서사적 '코어'(가정부가 손님을 살해한다는 사건)가 있으며 인과관계가 완전히 사라지지 않는다. *Wavelength*나 *Zorns Lemma*는 이야기 자체를 해체하지만 *Jeanne Dielman*은 이야기를 지속하면서 시간을 무기화한다. 따라서 50 배치.

**ETX에서의 중복 배치 문제**: *Histoire(s) du cinéma*와 *Jeanne Dielman*이 ETX와 ITX 양쪽에 등장한다. 이는 올바른 신호다 — 이 영화들은 다차원적으로 높은 접근 비용을 요구한다. 축은 독립적이지만 영화는 여러 축에서 동시에 높은 점수를 받을 수 있다.

**CTX 계단 구조**: Roy Andersson 삼부작은 CTX의 가장 명확한 계단형 사례다: Film 1 = 25 (스타일 개시), Film 2 = 50 (스타일 전제), Film 3 = 75–100 (철학적 페이로드는 삼부작 전체 축적이 필요). Kiarostami의 Koker 삼부작도 유사한 구조를 보인다.

**Hong Sang-soo의 CTX**: 홍상수 영화는 CTX 75-100에 독특한 위치를 차지한다. 그의 영화들은 캐릭터의 '연속성'이 아니라 상황·패턴·변주의 인식에 의존한다. 이는 다른 시리즈적 의존과 다른 종류의 관내(oeuvre) 의존성이다.

**Sátántangó의 다차원성**: Tarr의 *Sátántangó*는 FR=75, ETX=50, ITX=25에 배치된다. 이 영화는 형식적 급진성(7.5시간, 반복, 진흙)이 주된 비용이지만, 헝가리 포스트-공산주의 맥락도 거의 필수적이다. ITX 비용은 낮다 — 영화사 지식 없이도 정치적 절망을 읽을 수 있다.

**"어렵고 공허한" 영화 주의**: 배치 과정에서 높은 접근 비용을 정당화하기 위해 높은 예술적 가치를 암시하려는 편향을 의식적으로 배제했다. 형식적 급진성이 높아도 내용이 공허할 수 있다. 비용은 있지만 보상이 없는 영화도 존재하며, 이 축은 그것을 구별하지 않는다 — 그것은 다른 축(예: 독창성, 감동 깊이)의 역할이다.
