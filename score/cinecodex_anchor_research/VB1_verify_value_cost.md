## 축 혼동 (Axis Confusion)

1. **FORM cap is mis-evidenced, not mis-conceived.** The Luhrmann cap is the right *idea* (spectacle ≠ achieved form), but the cited Collider source praises Moulin Rouge's integration — so the file is using a "style IS substance" article to argue "style is NOT substance." Either re-source (find a genuine style-over-substance critique) or move Moulin Rouge up and keep The Great Gatsby (which has firmer style-over-substance consensus) as the cap exemplar.

2. **COG: difficulty smuggled in as value.** Funny Games (COG 75) over Parasite (COG 25) implies "makes the viewer work harder = more cognitive yield." That conflates *access cost* (which is A3's job) with *cognitive value*. Parasite delivers real ideas with low entry cost; that is precisely what COG 50 is for. Penalizing legibility is the inverse error the framework warns against.

3. **DUR vs FORM bleed.** Gravity at DUR 0 is justified in-note by "DUR doesn't measure FORM" — correct principle — but the placement still reads as a quality verdict ("emotional hollowness"). DUR 0 should mean "nothing recurs," which is empirically false for a film whose imagery is widely remembered. The note defends the axis while the placement violates it.

4. **A3 is the cleanest on this axis.** Its framing note (cost ≠ value) is rigorous and consistently applied — e.g., explicitly stating Histoire(s) being ITX/ETX/CTX-100 does NOT make it better than Bicycle Thieves. Good. No correction needed to A3's conceptual discipline; only its sourcing.

## 편향 공백 (Bias Gaps — specific films to add)

The corpus is overwhelmingly European + East Asian art-house + Hollywood. **Zero sub-Saharan African feature directors** (Supa Modo appears once in A3 ETX, a Kenyan film — good but isolated). **No South Asian cinema beyond Pather Panchali (A3 only).** **Latin America = only Cuarón/Meirelles/Guzmán.** **Women directors: Akerman, Sciamma, Wang, Labaki, Hardwicke — thin, and Akerman carries most of the load alone.**

Specific additions by band:

- **COG 75/100 (Africa):** *Touki Bouki* (1973, Djibril Diop Mambéty, Senegal) — fractured, citational form that reorganizes how viewers read African modernity. *Yeelen* (1987, Souleymane Cissé, Mali).
- **FORM 75/100 (Africa):** *Touki Bouki* again, or *Black Girl / La Noire de…* (1966, Ousmane Sembène) — foundational to African film grammar. *West Indies* (Med Hondo) — note Criterion itself just ran a major essay on it.
- **AFF 75/100 (South Asia / women):** *The Apu Trilogy* / *Pather Panchali* deserves an AFF, not only an A3 ITX, slot. *Fire* / *Earth* (Deepa Mehta). *The Cloud-Capped Star / Meghe Dhaka Tara* (1960, Ritwik Ghatak) — durable affective devastation.
- **MORAL 75/100 (Latin America / Africa):** *The Battle of Chile* is in A3 ETX but belongs in MORAL too. Add *Timbuktu* (2014, Abderrahmane Sissako, Mauritania/Mali) — moral complexity under jihadist occupation, near-unanimous critical respect; ideal *safe* anchor.
- **DUR / world canon (women):** *Daughters of the Dust* (1991, Julie Dash). *News from Home* or *Toute une nuit* (Akerman, to diversify beyond Jeanne Dielman). *Cléo from 5 to 7* (Varda) — conspicuously absent across all nine dimensions despite being a real-time-form landmark.
- **FR 50/75 (SE Asia / Latin America):** Weerasethakul and Lav Diaz are present (good). Add *Memoria* or a Lucrecia Martel film (*Zama*, *La Ciénaga*) — Martel is a glaring omission for a formal-radicalism axis and is maximally consensual among critics.

Highest-priority single fix: **Agnès Varda and Lucrecia Martel appear nowhere.** For a cinephile index that foregrounds women directors and formal innovation, that is indefensible.

## 합의 취약 앵커 (Consensus-Fragile Anchors — should be swapped)

Anchors must be SAFE. These are not:

1. **The Tree of Life (COG 100, DUR 100):** Genuinely divisive (Cannes boos + Palme d'Or). Replace at COG 100 with a consensual exemplar: *Mirror* (already used elsewhere), *Persona*, or keep *2001/Stalker/Shoah*. Do not anchor a top band on a film whose own cited review calls it a failure.

2. **Dancer in the Dark (AFF 100):** von Trier is the canonical "love it or hate it" director. Swap for *Grave of the Fireflies* (already present) or *Tokyo Story* as the safe AFF-100; demote Dancer to an *illustration* of the "provocation ≠ low affect" principle, not an anchor.

3. **Funny Games (COG 75):** Defensible reading but the film is explicitly designed to be alienating and is widely resented; "consensus across critical traditions" is shaky. Safer COG-75: *Close-Up* or *Sans Soleil* (both already in band). Keep Funny Games as discussion, not anchor.

4. **Son of Saul (MORAL 75):** The file's own cited source surfaces the prominent "exploitation film... because of its technical skill" critique. There is a real anti-camp. *The White Ribbon* or *No Country* (same band) are more consensual MORAL-75 anchors.

5. **Joker (COG 25):** Contested in the other direction — many critics rate it as having *no* cognitive yield (COG 0–floor) while fans overrate it. Volatile; a poor calibration point. Replace with something stably "one decent idea, conventionally delivered" (e.g., *The Truman Show*, *Children of Men* as idea-bearing-but-accessible).

6. **Moulin Rouge! (FORM 50 cap):** As shown in 출처 검증, even the cited source disagrees it's "uncontrolled." Fragile as a cap exemplar. *The Great Gatsby (2013)* carries firmer style-over-substance consensus; lead the cap with it.
