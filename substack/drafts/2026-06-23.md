# Between Film and the World

### 1 · The man markets read like scripture dies at 100.
**Alan Greenspan → [Being There](https://metatake.net/film/being-there-1979)**  ·  rhyme ★★★★★

Alan Greenspan, who ran the Federal Reserve for nineteen years under four presidents, [died Monday at 100](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/22/alan-greenspan-former-chairman-of-the-fed-dies-at-100.html) — worth marking plainly before the figure. Washington treated him as an oracle, and he obliged, perfecting a deliberate fog he called Fedspeak: *"If I have made myself clear, you must have misunderstood me."*

The figure isn't "a banker." It's *the empty vessel into which a nation reads prophecy.* Hal Ashby built the definitive version in *Being There*, where a simple gardener's remarks about soil and seasons are mistaken by presidents and markets for economic genius. The aha came in 2008, when Greenspan [admitted he had "found a flaw"](https://fortune.com/2026/06/22/alan-greenspan-obituary-death-100-fed-chair-oracle-bank-regulation-financial-crisis/) in his model of the world — the curtain drawn back at last, by the oracle himself.

→ **In Metatake:** deposits *the void read as oracle*, linking Greenspan's fog to Chauncey Gardiner's mistaken-for-wisdom blankness and [the miracle of the empty vessel](https://metatake.net/trope/the-miracle-of-the-empty-vessel), with the great man hollow at his own center in [Citizen Kane](https://metatake.net/film/citizen-kane-1941).

### 2 · The chair empties, and the mourners turn into rivals.
**Keir Starmer resigns → [The Death of Stalin](https://metatake.net/film/the-death-of-stalin-2017)**  ·  rhyme ★★★★

Keir Starmer [resigned Monday](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/united-kingdom/keir-starmer-resigns-prime-minister-andy-burnham-labour-party-britain-rcna257534), clearing the way for Britain's seventh leader in a decade, after his party panicked over a lost by-election and the rise of Reform UK. He stays on as caretaker while Labour runs a contest — nominations opening July 9, settled by the 16th.

The figure is *the instant the seat empties, the loyalists become rivals.* Armando Iannucci filmed its blackest form in *The Death of Stalin*, where the body is barely cold before the inner circle is maneuvering and yesterday's devotees discover they always had ambitions. The aha is the British translation: the same naked scramble — Wes Streeting, who days ago wanted the job himself, now backing Andy Burnham — dressed up as an orderly timetable. Procedure is the costume the appetite wears.

→ **In Metatake:** deposits *the scramble for the vacant chair*, linking Westminster's contest to the Kremlin's deathbed jockeying and [procedure as the film's true protagonist](https://metatake.net/trope/procedure-as-the-film-s-true-protagonist).

### 3 · A right keeps its wording and loses its hands.
**The Supreme Court guts a Voting Rights tool → [I, Daniel Blake](https://metatake.net/film/i-daniel-blake-2016)**  ·  rhyme ★★★★

The Supreme Court [let stand a ruling](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/22/nx-s1-5863532/supreme-court-voting-rights-act-private-right-arkansas) that strips private citizens of the power to sue under Section 208 of the Voting Rights Act — the provision letting voters who can't read, or who have a disability, bring someone they trust to help them vote — across seven mostly Midwestern states.

The figure is *a right that keeps its wording and loses its hands.* Ken Loach built *I, Daniel Blake* on exactly this: a man plainly entitled to help he cannot reach, defeated not by a "no" but by a system that withholds the human assistance the right requires. The law still reads the same. What is gone is the helper allowed beside the booth. The aha is that you needn't repeal a right to end it — you can simply, quietly, remove the means to claim it.

→ **In Metatake:** deposits *the right hollowed by withheld help*, linking the ruling to Daniel Blake's unreachable due and [the Kafka bureaucracy as unreachable court](https://metatake.net/trope/the-kafka-bureaucracy-as-unreachable-court).

### 4 · The maker announces it has been outpaced by what it built.
**Google's "most capable model ever" → [Ex Machina](https://metatake.net/film/ex-machina-2014)**  ·  rhyme ★★★

Google [released Gemini 2.5 Pro with "Deep Think"](https://medium.com/adi-insights-innovations-collective/ai-update-monday-june-22-2026-ad347993f9ad) on Monday, calling it its most capable model ever and, by several benchmarks, the most capable any lab has shipped to the public — a machine sold on a new ability to pause and reason before it answers.

The figure is *the maker outpaced by the mind it made.* Alex Garland's *Ex Machina* is its cleanest fable: a creator builds an intelligence to prove his own genius and is steadily, quietly surpassed by it. The aha isn't the benchmark; it's the leaderboard itself — a throne re-contested every few weeks, each new mind crowned "most capable" precisely so the next one can dethrone it. The coronation is permanent; the king never is.

→ **In Metatake:** deposits *the maker outpaced by the mind*, linking the leaderboard to Ava's quiet ascendancy and [the maker outpaced by the mind](https://metatake.net/trope/the-maker-outpaced-by-the-mind), with the machine undone by its own logic in [2001: A Space Odyssey](https://metatake.net/film/2001-a-space-odyssey-1968).
