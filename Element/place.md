To make this extensive catalog (originally 495 items) more robust and analytically useful for your project, I have refined it based on the following principles:	1. **Structural Enhancement:** The categories have been reorganized to improve analytical flow.	Most significantly, **Category VI** has been renamed to **"The Public Realm: Spectacle, Consumption, and Ritual"**.	Two new archetypes, **32. The Eatery** and **33. Spaces of Ritual and Memory**, have been added here to properly categorize restaurants, religious sites, cemeteries, and museums.	This brings the total number of core archetypes from 36 to 38.	2. **Consolidation and Clarity:** Overlapping or redundant items have been merged (e.g., consolidating similar 'haunted' locations or merging "The Final Girl's House" and "The Slasher House").	3. **Generalization of Specific Tropes:** Names tied to specific IPs or overly narrow concepts (e.g., "The Overlook Hotel," "The Yellow Brick Road," "Stepford Suburb") have been generalized (e.g., "The Isolated Grand Hotel," "The Mythic/Fated Path," "The Conformist Suburb") to increase their applicability across different films.	4. **Focus on Spatial Characteristics:** In **VII. 36. The False Utopia / Dystopia**, items describing societal systems (e.g., "The Totalitarian State") were removed to focus purely on the *spatial* manifestations of those systems (e.g., "The Panopticon").
